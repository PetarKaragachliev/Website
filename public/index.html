    How this profile is described

    RFM estimates body fat as 64 - 20 x (height / hipbone-level waist), plus 12 for the female formula category. The original adult study covered ages 20+, so this app withholds RFM at 18-19 while retaining other measurements. It is a population-based estimate, not a measurement of muscle or a validated muscular/skinny-fat classifier. No invented age correction or guaranteed error margin is used.

    The separate NICE midpoint-waist option uses waist-to-height screening bands only below BMI 35. Those bands are not applied to hipbone-level measurements. BMI follows the NHS adult-from-18 convention. Old saved waists retain their original midpoint protocol and must be remeasured before RFM. Unrounded inputs decide categories; displayed numbers are rounded. Your optional existing body-fat result remains separate and unverified.

    A PRACTICAL STARTING STRUCTURE

    eating occasions.

    Three or four meals can work. Four is an optional starting structure for spreading your existing energy and protein across the day, not a requirement for your goal.

    EXAMPLE ONLY / ADJUST TO YOUR DAY

      About four hours between meals is one possible routine, not established as healthiest for everyone. Adjust around hunger, waking hours, training, and medical guidance; do not force late meals to follow this example.

      Sports-nutrition guidance discusses distributing protein every 3-4 hours, but this does not prove an optimal meal interval for general health. Total intake, food quality, and consistency matter. This is not a food-by-food meal plan.

      02 / KNOW YOUR NUMBERS

      TRANSPARENCY OVER PROMISES
      01.

      Estimate, don't guess.

      Mifflin-St Jeor and activity estimate maintenance. Loss uses a rough 7,700 kcal/kg starting heuristic; gain uses modest surpluses linked to your chosen pace. Neither predicts actual weight change. Targets and pace don't guarantee health, muscle gain, or a completion date.

      Why weight-change estimates vary (opens in a new tab)↗
      02.

      Understand the limits.

      Automated nutrition targets are limited to ages 18-56, current and target BMI 18.5 to below 30, and computed targets of 1,500-4,500 kcal/day. Loss deficits above 20% of estimated maintenance are blocked. These are product limits, not a diagnosis or safe-intake guarantee. BMI doesn't distinguish fat from muscle; age 18 is outside the original equation sample.

      03.

      Keep the bigger picture.

      Macros are not a complete eating plan. Food quality, fibre, micronutrients, rest, and consistent training matter too. For weight extremes, medical needs, or unexplained changes, get individual guidance.
