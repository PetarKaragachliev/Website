# Exercise Media Preview

The training preview uses eight YouTube candidates selected from source-page descriptions and successful YouTube oEmbed metadata responses. They have not received visual technique review or qualified content approval. They are not a replacement for instruction. No video is downloaded, rehosted, autoplayed, or loaded before its button is pressed. Exercises without a matched candidate retain text guidance rather than an unrelated video.

YouTube uses youtube-nocookie.com and receives the site origin, not form fields, as the cross-origin referrer. Once loaded, the provider receives ordinary network/device data and can apply its own policies. Closing/changing the active day, editing inputs, regenerating, resetting or restoring the page removes video iframes.

## Gym Map Preview

Selecting gym automatically inserts a lazy Google Maps iframe below the plan/media. Home or no selection removes it. Its fixed query never contains form data or browser geolocation; referrer is disabled. Google still receives network/device data. There is no address entry field.

The single example is Athletic Fitness Markovo Tepe, Mall Markovo Tepe, Ruski Boulevard 52-54, Plovdiv, floor 3. Official source: https://athletic.bg/klubove/markovo-tepe/ . Exact Google listing: https://www.google.com/maps?cid=5854447715591440372 . This is a demonstration, not an exhaustive directory of Plovdiv gyms. Additional owner-supplied pins and a controlled multi-pin map remain follow-up work; the current single-place embed is not a multi-pin data store.

## Verification Limits

Automated provider fixtures verify URLs, layout, day-to-exercise mapping and iframe lifecycle, not third-party rendering or playback. Live Chromium loaded the real Maps iframe and exact gym listing; the listing identified the matching name, address and phone, but the iframe DOM exposed only map controls/copyright. Live Chromium loaded the leg-press YouTube player with the expected title/publisher and HTTP 200. Playback and visual technique were not reviewed. Other videos' playback, geography/device restrictions and actual multi-pin display are unverified. No screenshots, recordings or traces were generated.
